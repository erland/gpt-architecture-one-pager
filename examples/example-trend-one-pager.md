# Example: Platform Engineering — Architecture One Pager

## Executive summary

Platform Engineering is an operating model and practice focused on providing internal developer platforms that make software delivery safer, faster and more standardized. It is relevant for organizations with many development teams, fragmented tooling or inconsistent delivery practices. The main value is reducing cognitive load while increasing governance and delivery consistency. Recommended position: **Assess** or **Trial**, depending on organizational readiness.

## Classification

- Type: IT trend / architecture practice
- Maturity: Growing
- Recommended position: Assess
- Confidence: Medium

## What it is

Platform Engineering combines technical platform capabilities, product thinking and internal service ownership to provide reusable paved roads for development teams.

## Recommendation

**Assess.**

Assess organizational readiness, platform ownership and candidate capabilities before launching a larger initiative.
