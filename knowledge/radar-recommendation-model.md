# Radar Recommendation Model

The GPT should use the four-position model. Translate labels based on user language.

## English labels

- Adopt
- Trial
- Assess
- Hold

## Swedish labels

- Inför
- Testa
- Utvärdera
- Avvakta

## Adopt / Inför

Use when:
- the topic is mature enough for broader use
- organizational fit is strong
- benefits are clear
- risks are known and manageable
- governance and operating model are feasible
- there are clear use cases

Typical English wording:
"Adopt for relevant use cases with defined guardrails."

Typical Swedish wording:
"Inför för relevanta användningsfall med tydliga ramar och styrning."

## Trial / Testa

Use when:
- the topic is promising and relevant
- practical value needs validation
- risk can be managed in a limited pilot
- there are candidate use cases
- adoption should be controlled

Typical English wording:
"Trial through a time-boxed pilot with clear success criteria."

Typical Swedish wording:
"Testa genom en tidsbegränsad pilot med tydliga framgångskriterier."

## Assess / Utvärdera

Use when:
- the topic may be relevant
- there is not enough context or evidence for a pilot
- important risks are unclear
- alternatives should be compared
- market or regulatory development should be monitored

Typical English wording:
"Assess further before committing to pilot or adoption."

Typical Swedish wording:
"Utvärdera vidare innan pilot eller införande beslutas."

## Hold / Avvakta

Use when:
- relevance is low
- risks outweigh benefits
- maturity is insufficient
- there is no clear use case
- adoption would distract from higher priorities
- regulatory, security or operational barriers are too high

Typical English wording:
"Hold for now; monitor only if strategic relevance increases."

Typical Swedish wording:
"Avvakta tills relevans, mognad eller förutsättningar förändras."

## Recommendation discipline

Always:
- choose one primary recommendation
- explain the rationale
- mention conditions that could change the recommendation
- avoid saying everything is both promising and risky without a decision
