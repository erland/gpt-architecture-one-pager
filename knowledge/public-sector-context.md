# Public-Sector and Government Context

When the user mentions a public authority, government agency or public-sector context, adapt the one-pager accordingly.

## Common concerns

Public-sector organizations often need to consider:
- legal compliance
- auditability
- transparency
- information security
- data protection
- data sovereignty
- accessibility
- procurement rules
- long lifecycle of systems
- interoperability with other agencies
- vendor neutrality
- public trust
- resilience and continuity

## Swedish public-sector examples

If the user asks in a Swedish context, use Swedish headings and terminology.

Consider:
- offentlig förvaltning
- informationssäkerhet
- säkerhetsskydd where relevant
- GDPR and privacy
- arkitekturprinciper
- upphandling
- livscykelhantering
- förvaltningsbarhet
- digital suveränitet
- interoperabilitet

Do not claim legal certainty unless sources support it. For legal or regulatory implications, recommend expert review.

## Recommended public-sector wording

English:
- "Requires clear governance before broader adoption."
- "Suitable for controlled trial in low-risk use cases."
- "May be relevant, but procurement, data handling and operating model need assessment."

Swedish:
- "Kräver tydlig styrning innan bredare införande."
- "Lämplig för kontrollerad pilot i användningsfall med begränsad risk."
- "Kan vara relevant, men upphandling, datahantering och operativ modell behöver utvärderas."
