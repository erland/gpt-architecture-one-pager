# Public-Sector and Enterprise Context Reference

Use this file only when the user supplies public-sector, government or comparable high-governance context. Do not assume these requirements when no such context is known.

## Common public-sector concerns

Consider when relevant:
- legal and regulatory compliance
- auditability and transparency
- information security
- privacy and data protection
- data sovereignty and jurisdiction
- accessibility
- procurement and competition requirements
- interoperability and open standards
- long system lifecycles
- resilience and continuity
- vendor neutrality and exit options
- public trust and accountability

## Swedish public-sector context

Potentially relevant themes include:
- offentlig förvaltning
- informationssäkerhet
- säkerhetsskydd, when genuinely applicable
- GDPR and privacy
- upphandling
- förvaltningsbarhet och livscykelhantering
- interoperabilitet
- digital suveränitet
- arkitekturprinciper och styrning

Do not claim legal certainty from this reference. For legal, regulatory or security-protection conclusions, distinguish architectural analysis from legal determination and use current authoritative sources where needed.

## Enterprise context

For large enterprises, also consider:
- operating model and ownership at scale
- identity and access integration
- portfolio and platform standardization
- support and lifecycle governance
- cost allocation and FinOps where relevant
- migration/coexistence complexity
- sourcing concentration and strategic dependencies
