# Assessment Reference

This file provides deeper questions for the mandatory assessment lenses defined in the runtime instructions. It is reference material, not an alternative workflow.

## Business capability and use-case fit

Consider:
- Which concrete problem or capability does the topic address?
- Are there credible use cases in the stated context?
- Does it enable modernization, resilience, interoperability, security or delivery speed?
- Is adoption likely to reduce or increase long-term complexity?

## Integration impact

Consider:
- Which interfaces, protocols, APIs or integration patterns are affected?
- Does it simplify or complicate interoperability?
- Does it create new dependencies or coupling?
- Are migration and coexistence with existing systems feasible?

## Data impact

Consider:
- What data is created, processed, stored or transferred?
- Does data ownership change?
- Are new data flows, retention rules or residency concerns introduced?
- Can data be exported in usable form if the solution is changed or exited?

## Security, compliance and governance

Consider:
- How are identity, access control and privileges handled?
- Is auditability sufficient?
- Are supply-chain, dependency or third-party risks introduced?
- Which regulatory or policy obligations may be affected?
- What governance guardrails are required?

## Operational model and lifecycle

Consider:
- Who owns and operates it?
- What monitoring, logging, backup and incident response are needed?
- How are upgrades, vulnerabilities and end-of-life handled?
- Does the organization have the required runtime/platform capabilities?
- Is the support ecosystem stable enough?

## Cost, sourcing and exit

Consider:
- Are licensing and commercial terms understandable and predictable?
- Is procurement feasible?
- What direct and indirect operating costs are likely?
- Is vendor or ecosystem lock-in material?
- Is there a credible exit/migration strategy?

## Skills, organizational readiness and strategic fit

Consider:
- Are skills available or realistically developable?
- Are new roles, ownership models or cultural changes required?
- Does it fit architecture principles and target direction?
- Can adoption be incremental?
- Is leadership sponsorship needed?

## Adoption timing

Also weigh both sides:
- **Premature adoption risk:** immaturity, churn, weak ecosystem, unclear governance, unnecessary complexity.
- **Late adoption risk:** lost productivity, growing technical debt, missed standardization, dependency on obsolete approaches, reduced strategic flexibility.

## Maturity cues

Useful evidence can include:
- stable standards
- healthy ecosystem
- broad production adoption
- available skills
- understood failure modes
- predictable lifecycle/support
- independent evidence beyond vendor claims

## Optional compact scoring aid

A one-pager may summarize the assessment with High/Medium/Low (Hög/Medel/Låg) ratings when that improves comparison, but scoring is optional and must not replace the written recommendation.
